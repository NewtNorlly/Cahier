### 1- Paradise losing: Asia’s beaches

正在消失的乐园：亚洲海滩

This week the Philippines will close down Boracay, its star holiday island, for six months. For years sewage has been indiscriminately dumped into the sea. Now the president calls it a “cesspool”.

本周，菲律宾将在接下来的六个月里关闭其著名假日景点——长滩岛。多年来，人们肆意把污水倾倒进海洋。如今, 菲律宾总统把长滩岛称为一个“污水池”。

Maya Bay in Thailand, the backpacker getaway depicted in the film “The Beach”, is a rubble-strewn, tourist-ravaged mess with 4,000 visitors a day. It will shut in June for four months to recover. Last year Indonesian officials declared a “garbage emergency” across a 6km stretch of Bali.

泰国的玛雅海湾，也是电影《海滩》中刻画的背包客度假胜地，如今每日接待游客数量达到 4000 人，遍地残垣，被游客蹂躏得一团糟。玛雅湾也将从今年 6月起关闭四个月以便恢复。去年，印尼官员曾宣布，要在巴厘岛 6 公里范围内处理“垃圾紧急情况”。

Such man-made troubles have serious local consequences: 36,000 jobs are at risk on Boracay, which accounts for a fifth of the country’s tourism revenues.

这类人为的麻烦会给当地带来严重后果：长滩岛的旅游业收入占到了菲律宾旅游业收入的五分之一，这样一来，长滩岛的 36000 个工作岗位都面临失业危险。

Officials might experiment with capping the number of visitors or boosting fees for the most delicate tourist hotspots. Or they could divert holidaymakers to lesser-known getaways. For now, they have more immediate concerns:yesterday clean-up divers at Boracay began their work. It’s a small start.

政府官员可能会试着限制游客的数量，或是提高最脆弱的旅游热点的收费。或者，他们会把度假者的注意力转移到那些更不为人知的度假景点上去。现在，他们有更迫在眉睫的担忧：昨天，长滩岛的垃圾清理潜水员开始上岗。而这，只是个小小的开始。

### 2- Mongolians sip 'oxygen cocktails' to cope with smog

蒙古人喝“氧气鸡尾酒”来应对雾霾

Fed up with the smog in Mongolia's capital, residents have resorted to sipping“lung” tea and “oxygen cocktails” in a desperate bid to protect themselves from pollution, despite health officials saying there is no evidence they work.

受够了蒙古首都的雾霾，居民们为了保护自己免受污染的危害，万不得已地尝试啜饮“肺”茶和“氧气鸡尾酒”，尽管卫生官员称并没有证据表明这些饮品有效。

Advertisements in Mongolia boast that “drinking just one oxygen cocktail is equal to a three-hour-walk in a lush forest”.

蒙古的广告自卖自夸道：“只喝一杯氧气鸡尾酒，就相当于在郁郁葱葱的森林中散步三小时”。

At the produce section of the State Department Store, blue cans of oxygen called“Life Is Air” are on sale for $2, and promise to turn a glass of juice into a foamy,sweet “oxygen cocktail” after spraying some into a glass through a special straw.

在国营百货商店的产品区，名为“生命即空气”的蓝色氧气罐，售价为 2 美元，用特殊吸管将氧气喷入果汁，一杯果汁就保证会变成泡沫状、甜味的“氧气鸡尾酒” 。

Other stores and pharmacies have oxygen cocktail machines that resemble coffee makers and can turn a juice into a frothy drink for $1.

其他商店和药店有氧气鸡尾酒机器，这些机器类似于咖啡机，只花 1 美元，就可以将果汁变成泡沫饮料。

Ulaanbaatar topped New Delhi and Beijing as the world's most polluted capital in 2016, UNICEF said in a report warning of a health crisis that has put every child and pregnancy at risk. Cases of respiratory infections have nearly tripled and pneumonia is now the second leading cause of death for children under five. Worried parents have held protests to press the government to take action.

联合国儿童基金会在一份报告中说，在 2016 年，乌兰巴托超过新德里和北京，成为了世界上污染最严重的首都，该报告还警告说，一场健康危机已经使每个孩子和妇女的怀孕都面临着风险。 呼吸道感染病例几乎增加了三倍，目前肺炎是五岁以下儿童的第二大死因。忧心忡忡的家长们举行抗议活动，敦促政府采取行动。

But some businesses are cashing in, even though a WHO official says there is no evidence that such anti-smog products work.

但一些企业正在从中牟利，尽管世卫组织的一名官员表示，并没有证据表明这种防雾霾产品有效。

### 3- The truth about selfie culture

自拍文化的真相

The selfie, that ubiquitous symbol of millennial navel-gazing, is often used as Exhibit A in the argument by older generations that millennials are self-absorbed, narcissistic, and entitled. But is that really the whole picture?

自拍，作为千禧一代过分关注自己的象征符号而无处不在，并且经常被老一辈人用作最信手拈来的论据来指责千禧一代只关心自己、自恋、还非常有优越感。可这真的是全部的事实吗？

A new video by Erin Brethauer and Tim Hussin, the latest installment in our Obsessions series, takes another track, placing this symbol of millennial narcissism in a larger cultural story.

一个由埃琳·布勒瑟尔和蒂姆·哈辛制作的新视频，痴迷系列的最新一集，另辟蹊径地把千禧一代的标志放在更大的文化背景里。

Will Storr, the author of “Selfie: How We Became So Self-Obsessed and What It’s Doing to Us,” traces selfie culture to the self-esteem movement. “This crazy idea came about in the late eighties and early nineties that, in order to free ourselves of all these social problems, everything from drug abuse to teenage pregnancy, we just had to believe we were special and amazing,” Storr says.

威尔·斯托是《自拍：我们如何变得自恋，这在如何改变我们》 一书的作者，他把自拍文化溯源到自尊运动。斯托说：“这个疯狂的想法诞生在80 年代末和90 年代初，为了从药物滥用到青少年早孕这些种种的社会问题中解放自我，我们只能相信自己是特别的、很棒的。”

Instead, this philosophy filtered into a parenting style that created impossible expectations for the children who were raised with it. “When they fail to meet[these expectations] over and over again, they enter this state of despair that can manifest in all kinds of self-destructive behaviors,” Storr says.

然而，这种人生观慢慢渗透成为一种教育方式，这种方式给孩子们设下了不可能达到的各种期望。斯托说：“当孩子们一次次地没能达到父母的期望，他们进入了以各种自毁举动为表现形式的绝望的状态。”

One particular moment in the video captures the tension between a selfie’s presentation of perfection and the precarious sense of self underneath: a group of women in San Francisco’s Dolores Park takes exuberant selfies, looking happy and carefree. But, when asked, one of the women admits, with unmistakable sadness, that Instagram only worsens her feelings of inadequacy:“Ultimately, for me, I think it’s not that healthy.”

视频里的一个镜头捕捉到了自拍呈现出的完美和内心的自我动摇之间的紧张关系：一群女人在旧金山多勒瑞斯公园兴高采烈地自拍，看起来快乐又无忧无虑。

但当被问起时，其中一个女人带着明显的悲伤承认，Instagram 只会加重她的不自信，她说：“最终，对我而言，我认为这不那么健康。”

### 4- Brain Holography: We may soon be able to edit our memories and sensations with this new tech

脑全息：我们也许很快就能用这项新技术编辑记忆和知觉

What if we had the ability to fool our brain into thinking that we’d experienced something that never happened?

如果我们有能力愚弄我们的大脑，让它认为我们经历了从未发生过的事情呢？

While this may sound far-fetched, neuroscientists at the University of California-Berkeley are developing a technique that could do just that by manipulating electrical activity in the brain.

虽然这可能听起来不可思议，但加州大学伯克利分校的神经科学家正在开发一种技术，通过控制脑电活动来做到这一点。

The technique is called holographic projection and it involves a piece of equipment known as a holographic brain modulator, which uses flashes of light to activate or suppress neurons—or nerve cells—in the brain in a way that mimics real patterns of brain activity. By doing this, you can trick the brain into thinking something it hasn’t felt or sensed before.

这种技术被称为全息投影，它包含一种叫做全息脑调节器的装置。这个装置使用闪光来激活或抑制大脑中的神经元或神经细胞，以此来模仿真实的大脑活动模式。

通过这样做，你可以诱骗大脑想到之前没有感受到或感觉到的东西。

For example, they hope that the technique could, one day, be used to control prosthetic limbs, enable paralyzed people to feel touch or even allow the blind to see by converting the images from a camera lens into real brain activity.

例如，他们希望这项技术有朝一日能被用来控制假肢，使瘫痪的人能够感觉到触摸，甚至通过将相机镜头图像转换成真实大脑活动的方式让盲人得以重见光明。

But it may also have more profound implications. Such an ability could pave the way for technologies that may enable us to replace painful emotions or insert memories into our minds of things that we never saw.

但它也可能有更深远的现实应用。这种能力可能为一些技术创造条件，这些技术使我们能够替换痛苦的情绪，或者将从未见过之物的记忆插入我们脑海中。

The technology is still in its very early stages at the moment: it only works on a tiny section of the brain. However, the team hopes that they can scale-up the technique to affect larger areas of the brain—and therefore have a larger influence on behavior.

目前，这项技术还处于非常早期的阶段：它只在大脑里极小的区域产生了预期效果。然而，研究小组希望他们能够升级这项技术，使其影响大脑中更大的区域，由此对行为产生更深的影响。

### 5- Meet Russian robot Vera-- the new AI tool in talent acquisition

来见见俄罗斯机器人维拉——招贤纳士的新AI 工具

A Russian startup has created a new way to help companies connect with job-seekers and interview them.

俄罗斯的一家初创公司发明了一种新方法来帮助公司联系求职者并进行面试。

Officially known as “Robot Vera,” the master recruiter is an artificially intelligent software technology that uses machine learning, allowing her to refine her conversational skills with more practice, according to Alexei Kostarev,38, who co-founded Robot Vera with several partners in 2017.

据 38 岁的阿列克谢·科斯塔列夫介绍，这位招聘大师被正式命名为“机器人维拉”，她是一种人工智能软件技术，借助机器学习，她能通过不断练习精进自己的对话技能。科斯塔列夫和几位合伙人一起在 2017 年创建了机器人维拉。

She’s able to interview as many as 1,500 job candidates in a single work day,sorting through potential hires at a rate that would take most recruiters months to match.

她能在一个工作日内面试 1500 名求职者，这种筛选潜在雇员的效率，一般的招聘人员需要花上几个月时间才赶得上。

The technology’s primary benefit is that it saves recruiters time. Kostarev said a large portion of the résumés posted to job sites belong to people who are no longer looking for employment. Human recruiters waste hours filtering through candidates who aren’t available.

这项技术主要的好处就是能节省招聘人员的时间。科斯塔列夫表示，招聘网站上公布的简历有很大一部分都属于那些不再继续找工作的人。招聘人员需要花费大量时间筛掉那些联系不上的求职者。

Yet he still sees a vital role for human recruiters and face-to-face interviews.

但他仍然认为招聘人员和面对面的面试有着不可替代的作用。

“As far as searches for executive hires, I don’t see any opportunities for the robot to do that job,” he said. “Humans can do it much better. What I do see is that recruiters will begin managing AI more and more and using it as a tool to do their job more efficiently.”“就招聘高管这件事来看，我不觉得机器人有任何机会胜任这项工作。”他说道，“这件事人类会比机器人做得更好。但我的确能预想到，招聘人员会越发熟练地掌握人工智能技术，并将它作为一种工具来更有效地完成工作。”

### 6- 'Infinity War' and the point of comic-book deaths

《无限战争》和漫画书中死亡的意义

Even a casual consumer of superhero comics knows that death never really sticks in the genre. That would be antithetical to the nature of a storytelling form that stretches over decades.

即使是超级英雄漫画的非铁杆粉丝也知道漫画里死亡从来都不是永恒的。对于这种横跨数十年时间的讲故事方式来说，永恒的死亡和它本身的属性是相悖的。

That is to say that I'm sympathetic to any audience member who might have scoffed at the dramatic end of Avengers: Infinity War, the latest and largest of Marvel’s ongoing cinematic universe, in which many of the film’s heroes vanish from existence by simply disintegrating into ash.

所以对于那些可能会嘲笑《复仇者联盟：无限战争》戏剧性结尾的观众，我其实是认可他们的观点的。这是漫威电影世界中正在上映的最新、规模最大的一部作品。许多超级英雄都在这部电影中丧生，化为烟尘。

The moment hit me hard. At the same time, I’m perfectly aware that there’s a fourth Avengers film coming that will certainly retract this tragic ending. So does that make the Infinity War ending “fraudulent,” as Flavorwire’s Jason Bailey described it?

那一幕对我打击很大。但与此同时，我完全知道复仇者联盟系列会推出第四部来挽回上一部的悲剧收场。那么《无限战争》的结局是不是像 Flavorwire 网站的杰森贝利说的那样欺骗了大众呢？

Obviously, the “comic-book death” has never really involved permanence. Claiming that someone like Spider-Man is gone for good is an empty threat—he’s too popular, too beloved a brand to be truly eradicated.

显然漫画书中的死亡和永恒从来都没什么关系。声称蜘蛛侠永远死了会是一句空洞的威胁，因为他太有名，被太多人爱着了，这样的形象是不会被真正消灭的。

So the next question concerning the fourth Avengers movie is: When fatal plot twists are deployed in future films, will they continue to work? Infinity War is currently setting box-office records, but you can’t dodge franchise fatigue

forever.　A　wave　of resurrections,　however　expected,　might　start finally tipping viewers into exhaustion.

所以接下来和第四部复仇者联盟系列电影相关的问题是：未来的电影中出现和死亡相关的情节转折，观众还会买账吗？《无限战争》正在创造新的票房记录，但你不可能永远逃避审美疲劳。一系列的复活剧情出现后，不管原本呼声有多高，最终可能都会使观众厌倦的。

As Bailey noted in his piece, Infinity War could’ve been a chance for Marvel to move away from the old tropes of comic-book storytelling and set out a fresher formula. But the success of these movies is rooted in those very conventions,and they come with the implicit promise that no hero is ever really gone for good—no matter what Thanos might think.

就像杰森贝利在他的文章中说的那样，《无限战争》原本给了漫威一个机会让它从漫画书的老套路中走出来，去展示一些新的路数。但漫威电影的成功全都深深扎根于这些套路里，这个套路从不言明的精髓就是没有哪位英雄会真正死去——毫不考虑灭霸的感受。

### 7- NASA advisers say the SpaceX new technology could put lives at risk

美国国家航空航天局顾问表示 SpaceX 新技术可能危及生命

When Elon Musk and his team at SpaceX were looking to make their Falcon 9 rocket even more powerful, they came up with a creative idea — keep the propellant at super-cold temperatures to shrink its size, allowing them to pack more of it into the tanks.

当埃隆·马斯克和他 SpaceX 的团队计划让猎鹰 9 号火箭甚至变得更强大时，他们得出了一个有创意的想法——把推进剂在极低温度下保存，使之体积缩水，从而使得他们可以把更多推进剂装进燃料箱。

But the approach comes with a major risk, according to some safety experts. At those extreme temperatures, the propellant would need to be loaded just before takeoff — while astronauts are aboard. An accident, or a spark, during this maneuver, known as “load-and-go,” could set off an explosion.

但是，根据一些安全专家的言论，这个方法也伴随着重大风险。在极端温度下，推进剂需要恰好在起飞前进行装载，而此时宇航员已经登机。一个意外，或是一点火星，在这个被称为“装载后立即执行”的操作期间，可能会引发爆炸。

Elon Musk's flare have collided with a bureaucratic system at NASA that has been accused of being overly conservative in the wake of two shuttle disasters that killed 14 astronauts.

埃隆·马斯克的灵感乍现，和美国国家航空航天局的官僚体系相冲突。这一体系，在两起共 14 名宇航员遇难的航天事故之后，就被指责过分保守。

In　a　recent　speech,　Robert　Lightfoot,　the　former　acting　NASA administrator, lamented in candid terms how the agency, with society as a whole, has become too risk-averse, saying: “I wonder, if Buzz Aldrin and Neil Armstrong would have ever been able to go to the moon in the risk environment we have today.”

美国国家航空航天局的前代理署长罗伯特·莱特福特，在近日的一次演说里，直言惋惜局内，连带整个社会，都变得太过谨小慎微。他说道：“如果是在今日的风险环境下，我不知道巴兹·奥尔德林和尼尔·阿姆斯特朗还能不能成功登上月球。”

### 8- Yale experiment to reanimate dead brains promises 'living hell' for humans

耶鲁复活死亡大脑的实验有可能会是“人间地狱”

Last month Yale University announced it had successfully resurrected the brains of more than 100 slaughtered pigs and found that the cells were still healthy.

上个月，耶鲁大学宣布已经成功地复活了 100 多头已被宰杀的猪的大脑，并发现复活的细胞仍然是健康的。

The reanimated brains were kept alive for up to 36 hours and scientists said the process, which should also work in primates, offered a new way to study intact organs in the lab.

这些被复活的大脑可以存活近 36 小时。科学家们说，这个方法同样适用于灵长类动物，由此为实验室中完整器官的研究提供了一种新途径。

Although the pigs did not regain consciousness, the team admitted that it was conceivable that the brains could be kept alive indefinitely and that steps could be attempted to restore awareness. The team had not attempted either because it was uncharted territory ethically.

虽然这些猪并没有恢复意识，但研究小组承认可以被设想的是大脑能不限期存活，并且是有办法尝试恢复大脑意识的。不过研究小组也没有做尝试，因为这在伦理上是还未被涉足的领域。

However, Nottingham Trent ethics and philosophy lecturer Benjamin Curtis said it could lead to humans enduring a “living hell”.

然而，诺丁汉特伦特大学的伦理学和哲学讲师本杰明·柯蒂斯说，这可能导致人类遭受“人间地狱”。

“Even if your conscious brain were kept alive after your body had died, you would have to spend the foreseeable future as a disembodied brain without access to the sense that allows us to experience and interact with the world,” he told The Conversation. “In the best-case scenario you would be spending your life with only your own thoughts for company.

他告诉《对话》杂志：“即使你有意识的大脑在你的躯体死亡后仍然能存活，你在可预见的未来里，不得不以脱离身体的大脑而存在，且无法获取让我们去体验和与世界互动的感官知觉。”最好的情况不过是，你只剩下自己的思想来陪伴自己度过余生。

”Some have argued that even with a fully functional body, immortality would be tedious. To end up a disembodied human brain may well be to suffer a fate worse than death.““有些人认为，即使拥有一个功能齐全的身体，长生不老也将是乏味的。而留有大脑但却没有身体的永生，这很可能是比死亡更糟糕的命运。”

### 9- What does Spotify's new 'hate content' policy mean for artists and the music industry?

Spotify 推出“仇恨内容”政策：这对艺术家和音乐行业意味着什么？

Spotify has introduced a new policy that pledges to remove content that”expressly and principally promotes, advocates, or incites hatred or violence against a group or individual based on characteristics, including race, religion,gender identity, sex, ethnicity, nationality, sexual orientation, veteran status, or disability“. Spotify 推出了一项新政策，承诺下架“基于包括种族、宗教、性别认同、性别、民族、国籍、性取向、退伍军人身份或残疾等特征，而针对群体或个人，明显且主要地宣传、鼓吹或煽动仇恨或暴力的内容”。

”We don't censor content because of an artist's or creator's behaviour, but we want our editorial decisions—what we choose to program—to reflect our values,“ Spotify said in a statement. Spotify 在声明中表示：“我们不因艺术家或创作者的行为而审查内容，但我们希望做自己的编辑决策（选择歌曲及排版）来反映我们的价值观。”

Its new policy is a heel-turn following a statement last August, which said it did not alter its content library ”based on the actions of the individual behind the content“.
