**1. My name is Mary Alice Young.**

我叫玛丽·爱丽丝·杨

**2. When you read this morning's paper, you may come across an article**

当你读到今早的报纸时，可能会看到一篇文章，

**3. about the unusual day I had last week.**

讲的是我上周度过的那个不寻常的一天

**4. Normally, there's never anything newsworthy about my life.**

平时，我的生活从来没有什么值得一提的

**5. But that all changed last Thursday.**

但上周四，一切都变了

**6. Of course, everything seemed quite normal at first.**

当然，一开始一切看起来都很正常

**7. Waffles? I made breakfast for my family.**

华夫饼？我给家人做了早餐

**8. I performed my chores.**

我做了家务

**9. I completed my projects.**

我完成了我的项目

**10. I ran my errands.**

我去跑了腿

**11. In truth, I spent the day as I spent every other day,**

说实话，我像往常一样度过那一天，

**12. quietly polishing the routine of my life**

静静地打磨着我生活的日常，

**13. until it gleamed with perfection.**

直到它闪耀着完美

**14. That's why it was so astonishing**

正因如此，才如此令人震惊，

**15. when I decided to go to my hallway closet**

当我决定走到走廊的壁橱前，

**16. and retrieve a revolver that had never been used.**

取出一把从未用过的左轮手枪

**17. My body was discovered by my neighbor, Mrs. Martha Hoover,**

我的尸体被邻居玛莎·胡佛太太发现，

**18. who had been startled by a strange popping sound.**

她被一声奇怪的爆裂声吓了一跳

**19. Her curiosity aroused.**

好奇心被勾了起来

**20. Mrs. Hoover tried to think of a reason**

胡佛太太试图想一个理由，

**21. for dropping in on me unannounced.**

好不请自来地顺道拜访我

**22. After some initial hesitation,**

在起初的一番犹豫之后，

**23. she decided to return the blender**

她决定归还那台搅拌机，

**24. she had borrowed from me six months before.**

那是她六个月前向我借的

**25. Mrs. Hoover The Thank you.**

胡佛太太 谢谢
