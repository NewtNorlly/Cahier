影视英语台词发音精读课堂笔记

（按老师逐句精读讲授的原有顺序整理，供复习、回顾与复盘）

总纲：贯穿全篇的发音主线（先记住，再逐句套用）

发声基调。老师几乎每一讲都强调：先把声音沉下去，从胸口以下、丹田的位置发声，不要沿用中文习惯的浅喉发音。方法是先正常发“啊”，再把注意力放在发声部位，想象它一点点下沉到丹田，当感到胸腔深处在震动时位置就对了。古典剧里许多词要发得比音标更深更圆，带出贵族腔调。（Lady Mary、战争与和平、南方与北方几讲反复讲这一点。）

四类核心技巧反复出现，几乎每句都要标，整理时按这四栏来记。一是连读：前一个词尾与后一个词首拼在一起读。二是失去爆破（吞音）：多为t、d、p、k 在词尾只做口型、不发声。三是重音：要强调的词读重读长，虚词读轻读短。四是语调：分升调、降调、先升再降、先降再升、弧线形（起—平—降）等，句尾常拐弯。

虚词弱读规律。to 常弱读成轻t 或轻/ə/；for 常弱读成/fə/；and 常只发一个/n/；the、a、of、in、have、has 这些虚词常常“机不可闻”，只带气声快速滑过。

英式贵族与古典剧发音特色。句尾爱拐弯（升降升降、先降再升），尾音爱往上升；鼻音偏重，像bed、on 这类词要感到鼻子偏上方在震动；元音发得深而圆，有时形容成“像有人要把一个鸡蛋往喉咙里塞”。

第1 讲都铎宫廷选段：恢复王位继承权（succession）原文台词：Elizabeth! Are you dressed? — Not quite. I was reading. Come and sit. I have some news.Mary, what is it? I just heard, by Act of Parliament, you and I are both restored to the succession after Edward and his heirs. Of course, the King himself must have commanded this. Are you not pleased, Elizabeth? Think what this means. Yes, Mary, if you are pleased,then so am I.逐句精读：

1. Are you dressed?连读：are you 两个音，重音在dressed。

语调：前两个音（are、you）是平调，发音部位较高，有点像捏着嗓子在说话；到第三个音dressed 先升再降。

2. Not quite.用法：口语里常用的省略表达，完整形式是I'm not quite dressed，意思是“还没完全穿好”；quite 修饰dressed，表示“完全地”。

细节：因为说话人语速很慢，所以没有把第一个t 吞掉，t 是发出来的。

3. I was reading.语调：I was 先降再升，reading 先升再降，整体是弧线式的读法。

语法：用过去进行时，是为了紧接前面，解释“为什么还没穿好”——强调过去某个时刻正在进行的动作。

4. Come and sit.连读：come and 连在一起读；come 里带一个先降再升的调，显得语气活泼。

吞音：and 的d 吞掉，直接和后面的sit 连起来。sit 是很活泼的读法。看上去只有三个词，实际用到的读法很多。

5. I have some news.重音：把重音落在最后一个词news 上即可。

6. Mary, what is it?语调：Mary 先降再升。

连读：what 与is 连、is 与it 连，中间明显有两处连读。

7. You and I are both restored to the succession after Edward and his heirs.语调：you and I are both 读到both 时稍微往上扬；to 是清晰读出来的。

词汇：succession 是名词“继承权”；常用搭配be next in succession to the throne，如the prince is next in succession to the king，王子是王位的下一位顺位继承人。

8. Are you not pleased, Elizabeth?语调：前三个词（are you not）语调一致；因为带惊讶情绪，语调再次提高。

吞音：not 的t 吞掉；pleased 音调持续往上走。

9. If you are pleased, then so am I.细节：pleased 稍微拉长一点再收尾。

词汇与注释：succession 继承权；be next in succession to 是……的下一位顺位继承人。整段情绪是惊讶里带欣喜，所以多处语调上扬。

第2 讲古典登门选段：冒昧来访、约晚饭（sent me down）原文台词：I do hope I'm not interrupting. — Lady Mary. — Cousin Mary, please. Mamma has sent me down to welcome you and to ask you to dine with us tonight, unless you're too tired.— We would be delighted. — Good, come at eight. Won't you stay and have some tea? —Oh no, you're far too busy, and I wouldn't want to push in.逐句精读：

1. 发声准备（读Lady Mary 之前先做）方法：要把声音完全沉下去，整个人声音很稳、发音部位比较靠下。先用正常声音发“啊”，再把意识放在发声部位上，想象它一点点沉到丹田；当感到胸腔非常深的地方在震动时，位置就找到了。

2. I do hope I'm not interrupting.重音：do、hope 两个音都要加重。

连读：not 和interrupting 连起来读；把in 往上升。你会发现它很多尾音都喜欢往上升。

3. Mamma has sent me down to welcome you连读：has 和sent 直接连在一起；sent 的t 吞掉。

细节：down 嘴不要张那么大，把气沉下去；down 后面的音要“滑着”连过去，不是硬碰在一起发出来。welcome 的well 音加重一点。

连读：you 有一点往上升，并直接和后面的and 连在一起；and 的d 吞掉，后面的to 很轻很快带过去。

4. to ask you to dine with us tonight连读：ask you 两个音连在一起；dine 加重音；with us 两个音很快连在一起。

语调：tonight 先降再升，和前面interrupting 的尾音是同样的读法。

5. unless you're too tired.语调：tired 尾音也是往上挑的读法。

6. you're far too busy细节：far too 的o 嘴没张很大；因为语气在“阴阳怪气”，far 和too 两个都加重音。busy 是非常有特色的降声惯用读法。

7. and I wouldn't want to push in.连读：busy 后面的and 连在一起；wouldn't、want 两个音都往上升；want to 只读一个t。

细节：push in 继续阴阳怪气，要加重，并记得连读在一起。

词汇与注释：sent me down 派人（到乡下来）；welcome 欢迎；dine with us 与我们共进晚餐；unless you're too tired 要是你不太累；push in 打扰、挤进来。这段后半句要读出“阴阳怪气”的客套味。

第3 讲权臣宫廷选段：床上的把柄、伦敦八卦（Wolsey, London gossip）原文台词：I have something to show you. — Please don't give it currency. — This was in my bed.The sickly, milk-faced creeper had turned down the sheet. — Of course, I can't get any sense out of her. She cries if you look at her sideways. So I don't know who put it there.— I'm told that Wolsey kept you because you always knew the London gossip. If you find out who's responsible for this, I want you to tell me.逐句精读：

1. I have something to show you.弱读：I have 直接缩成I've；something to 的to 非常轻，快速滑过去。

重音：把重音放在后面的show 上。

2. This was in my bed.连读：this 稍微往上升一点，this、was、in 连在一起读。

发音：bed 先升再降（但轻微）；bed 的a 鼻音较重，要感到鼻腔上端有一点震动；尽量把嘴缩小，bed 的d 让舌头和牙齿做一点摩擦。

3. The sickly, milk-faced creeper had turned down the sheet.重音：sickly、milk、faced、creeper 四个词都读得重，咬字再狠一些。

吞音：had 的d 吞掉；turn down 中间有一处连读。

4. Of course, I can't get any sense out of her.细节：of course 先轻读、再上升；can't 的t 吞掉。

连读：get any 连；sense out 连；out of 连——三个词连成一串。

5. She cries if you look at her sideways.连读：look at 连；at her 连——三个音直接连在一起。

语调：sideways 先升再降。

6. So I don't know who put it there.连读：so I 两个词虽独立，但读得极快，像连在一起、直接滑过去；don't 的t 吞掉。

语调：don't know 两个音往上升；put 加一个重音，并和it 连在一起；there 最后一个音把调降下来。

7. I'm told that Wolsey kept you because you always knew the London gossip.细节：told 里to 往上升；that 的t 吞掉；人名Wolsey 重读；kept you 有轻微先降再升。

节奏：后面几个词调子都比较平缓，直到最后一个gossip 再把重音加上去。

8. If you find out who's responsible for this, I want you to tell me.细节：find 往上升；find out 连在一起后两个音都加重；responsible 重音在中间音节。

弱读：for 轻快带过去；this 直接升上去。

吞音：want to 的to 吞掉；want 发得很克制，嘴缩起来，不是张大嘴的读法。

tell me 先升再降。

词汇与注释：give it currency 把它传开、让它流传；creeper 文中是蔑称（令人讨厌的人）；get no sense out of her 从她嘴里问不出任何话；sideways 斜着眼；gossip 八卦八卦；Wolsey沃尔西（权臣人名）。

第4 讲宫廷侍女选段：女侍、恳求、效忠（lady-in-waiting, loyalties）原文台词：How merciful the king has been! You will serve as my lady-in-waiting. You will help me choose dresses and jewels and comb out my hair before I sleep. It'll be just like when we were girls, except that mother is imprisoned and father is dead. — Mother is not in prison;she is in sanctuary. You see, mother keeps writing to me, begging me to help her. — Has she helped you when father was defeated? — Mother writes to me too, daily and ever more beseechingly. The king must be in no doubt as to where our loyalties lie.逐句精读：

1. How merciful the king has been!重音：merciful 中间加一个重音。

弱读与节奏：the、has 读轻读快；把king 和been 吐出来。整体是“起—平—降”的完整弧线读法，最后三个词也是这种弧线。

2. You will serve as my lady-in-waiting.连读：lady 和in 之间加一处连读，所以后面三个词的节奏比前面稍快一点。

语调：同前句，弧线式读法。

3. You will help me choose dresses and jewels吞音：help 的p 吞掉；两个and 的d 都吞掉。

语调：dresses、jewels 两个音往上升。

4. and comb out my hair before I sleep.发音：comb 虽有b 结尾，但b 不发音；m 的音和out 连在一起；out 的t 也吞掉。

连读：before 和I 连读；before I 读快了很像卷舌音，其实是连读。

5. It'll be just like when we were girls,吞音：just 的t 吞掉、like 的k 吞掉；just 加一个重音。

弱读：when we were 轻而快地带过去，重音落在最后一个词girls。

6. daily and ever more beseechingly.语调：beseechingly 往上；to（这里指to 的弱读）发成轻t、快轻读过去。

重音：最后三个词因为要强调内容，都加了重音。

7. The king must be in no doubt as to where our loyalties lie.重音：the king 加重；must 的t 吞掉；be 和in 直接连读。

语调：no doubt 两个音都往上升；where 也往上升；loyalties 先升再降再升，直到最后的lie 才把音降下来。

词汇与注释：lady-in-waiting（贵族）侍女；merciful 仁慈的；sanctuary 避难所、圣所（此处指避难之地）；beseechingly 恳求地、哀求地；be in no doubt as to where our loyalties lie对我们要效忠谁毫不含糊。

第5 讲牧师宅邸选段：姐姐订婚、热闹的牧师住宅（rectory,engagement）原文台词：Eliza, you must find it in your heart to forgive the tardiness of my reply to your letter. Are these pretty jewels? Yes, Mama. We have arrived back at Steventon. But the truth is, our once peaceful rectory has been consumed by such a riot of celebration that it's hard to find a quiet place in which to write. There is so much joy and delight about as to make me feel quite sick and wicked. I cannot quite remember how we passed our time in the days before my sister's engagement. If there is one drawback to this perfect arrangement, it is that I will somehow have to live without her. I am not ready to let my beloved sister go.逐句精读：

1. There is so much joy and delight about重音：so much 重读。

语调：joy 稍微往下降一点；about 往下降。

连读：and 和delight 之间有一个连读（两个d 重合在一起读）；to about 之间也有一个连读。

2. as to make me feel quite sick and wicked.吞音：quite 的t 吞掉。

连读与重音：sick 和and 连在一起；把sick 和wicked 两个词加重音。

3. I cannot quite remember how we passed our time in the days连读：passed 和后面的our 连在一起（passed 加重音）；time 先升再降，比较轻微。

弱读：in the 很快很轻地带过去；days 加重音。

4. If there is one drawback to this perfect arrangement,重音：one drawback 两个重音；perfect 重音。

连读：drawback 与后面的in（to this）连在一起；arrangement 稍微往上升一点。

5. it is that I will somehow have to live without her.连读：that I 两个词连在一起。

语调：somehow 先升再降（轻微）；without her 先升再降，且两词中间是连在一起的。

6. I am not ready to let my beloved sister go.吞音：not 的t 吞掉；后面的to 也读成轻t。

发音：go 读得深一点、长一点，注意是饱满的/ɡəʊ/，不是短促的读法。

词汇与注释：tardiness 迟缓、拖延；rectory（英式）牧师住宅；Steventon 斯蒂文顿；a riot of celebration 一派欢腾；drawback 美中不足、缺点；engagement 订婚；beloved 心爱的。

第6 讲战争与和平选段：拒婚、瓦西里亲王（never to leave you）原文台词：What is it — yes or no? — Father, my wish is never to leave you. I don't want to marry.— What nonsense is this? Come here, girl! — Princess, ... I do hope I haven't offended you in any way. May I not return and try again? — Prince Vasili! Well, I've said all that I feel in my heart. I thank you for the honour, but I shall never be your son's wife.逐句精读：

1. 发声基调（学本段前先来一遍）定调：把发音基调先定好，声音往下沉，从胸口以下的部位发声。以第一个词father 为例：按中文习惯即使音标读对，发出来的也是平的father；把声音沉下去之后，才是地道的father。仔细体会两者区别，定好调再往下学。

2. Father, my wish is never to leave you.连读：wish 和is 之间有一个连读。

弱读：never to 的to，把o 音发成接近e 的音。

语调：leave you 稍微往上升一点点。

3. I don't want to marry.吞音与连读：don't 把后面的t 吞掉；want 和to 直接连在一起。

发音：marry 最后那个o 读得非常深，感觉像有人要把一个鸡蛋塞进喉咙里，喉咙往后缩；marry 里再加一处连读。

4. Prince Vasili.语调：结尾（Vasili）稍微往上升一点点。

5. I do hope I haven't offended you in any way.细节：haven't 的h 其实还是发出来的，但和前面I 的口型直接融在一起、很快带过去（不是I have 分开读）。

连读：I've said 的said 很快和前面连；that 和all 直接连；feel 和in 也有一处连读。

重音：heart 注意收声、重读。

6. I thank you for the honour,连读：thank 和you 连在一起。

弱读：for 不读for、弱读带过（和to 是一个道理）；the honour 的the 读/ði/，因为后面是元音。

7. but I shall never be your son's wife.语调：never 有一点点往上升，但后面son's 又急转直下往下降，读重一点。

词汇与注释：offend 冒犯；in any way 丝毫；May I not return and try again 我能不能回来再试一次；Prince Vasili 瓦西里亲王；honour 荣幸；son's wife 儿媳妇。

第7 讲海边回忆选段：惠特斯塔布尔、生蚝（Whitstable, oysters）原文台词：I had come so far from the days when I was a girl, standing on this beach, wondering why I didn't care for Freddie like I should. And here I am again, and though I shan't stay long,there's a part of me will always belong here in Whitstable, where they have the best oysters in the world.逐句精读：

1. I had come so far from the days弱读：I had 缩成I'd，把had 的音吞掉；so far 两个音都加重音。

弱读与连读：from the 非常轻非常快地带过去，重点落在days 上；when I 有一处连读；was a 也有一处连读。

2. when I was a girl standing on this beach连读：standing 的后鼻音和on 之间是滑音，standing on 连着走。

3. wondering why I didn't care for Freddie like I should.连读：wondering 把后面两个音直接连在一起读。

发音：why I 的嘴是从小稍微张大一点把这两个音发出来，不是嘴先合上再另发I。

重音：把重音放在care、Freddie、should 三个读音上。

4. And here I am again,连读：and here 似连非连，and 再和后面的I 连在一起。

发音：am 发得深一点。

5. though I shan't stay long,语调：though I 有一处升调，stay long 又有一处降调——这句最明显的就是先升再降。

6. there's a part of me will always belong here连读：part 和of 之间有一处连读；me 稍微往上升一点。

发音：always 发得很深，感觉额头都有一点在震动。

7. in Whitstable, where they have the best oysters in the world.重音：best 这里加一个重音。

发音：world 最后一个词读得更深，深到像声音都快下降到胃里去了。

词汇与注释：come so far from the days 离当年的日子已经很遥远；care for Freddie like I should 本该像应有的那样去喜欢Freddie；Whitstable 惠特斯塔布尔（地名）；belong here 属于这里；oysters 牡蛎。

第8 讲庄园质问选段：孩子是谁的、卖去热那亚（slave market in Genoa）原文台词：You may think that you can keep your position here, that your baby will never go hungry,if it's Marco Bellows. — How could it be Marco Bellows's? — You seem to have been abandoned. — I know what goes on in my own household, and it's no secret that you and he were close. If it were his, I would have told him, and he would have taken me with him.But I could do no such thing, because he always treated me with respect. — Thank you for your candour, but if you ever speak to me like that again, you'll be on your way to the slave market in Genoa. Get back to work!逐句精读：

1. How could it be连读与重音：could 前面的元音加一个重音；could it 之间连读。

2. Marco Bellows重音：Marco 这个重音加重一些；Bellows 先降一点点、再升一点点。

3. I know what goes on in my own household重音：I 放重音；what 吞掉。

连读：goes on 中间有连读；on 和in 之间也有连读。

发音：own 读得非常圆滑，三个音滑着连在一起。

4. and it's no secret that you and he were close连读与重读：it's、no 连在一起；no 重读；you 加一个重读，同时把he 和close也加重音。

节奏：整体节奏是the the the you and he were close，中间and 的d 吞掉。

5. If it were his, I would have told him要点：这句很短，但吞了四个音、连了四个音。if it 连读、it 的t 吞掉；were 的z 吞掉，慢读像a fei war his；his 和后面的I 也连读；would have 的h 不发音、直接和前面连；told him 的h 不算完全吞掉，而是和told 有一点点融在一起。

6. But I could do no such thing,要点：这句除了吞了一个t 和一个d 之外，其他都读得比较清晰。

7. because he always treated me with respect.重音：he 加一个重音；with respect 读清晰。

8. but if you ever speak to me like that again, you'll be on your way to the slave market in Genoa.吞音与弱读：speak 的k 吞掉，to 换成轻/ə/ 的音；like 的k 吞掉；that 和again之间也有连读；again 里的to 同样读成轻t。

连读：market 和in 之间有一处连读。

词汇与注释：Marco Bellows 人名；be abandoned 被抛弃；household 家宅、家里；close 关系亲密；if it were his 虚拟语气（要是这孩子是他的）；candour 坦率直言；slave market in Genoa 热那亚的奴隶市场。

第9 讲厨房对话选段：欧姆蛋与炒蛋（omelet, scrambled eggs）原文台词：I've got eggs, mushrooms. I've stopped eating onions now that you've left ham. I could make an omelet. — Great. — It's impossible. Darren usually leaves me notes, sticky notes with instructions. — I see. — Never mind, the menu's changed. I'm having scrambled eggs.— Good. Is that enough? — That's perfect.逐句精读：

1. I've got eggs, mushrooms吞音与弱读：got it 一带几乎像没发出来，只留一点音的感觉。

语调：mushrooms 整个调比较平，说明只是在筛选东西，并且有一种刻意拖长的音调。

2. I've stopped eating onions now that you've left ham.重音：stopped 的重音在中间那个o 元音上。

语调：onions 很特别，先升再降再升。

弱读与重音：now that 也非常轻；最后四个词里唯一稍重的就是left，而且只重在元音上。

3. I could make an omelet.吞音：could 的d 吞掉。

连读：make 和an 之间连读；an 和omelet 之间也连读。omelet 先升再降再升。

4. It's impossible.弱读与连读：it's 极轻、极快，it's 的s 和后面impossible 连在一起，带着it's 轻快地滑过去。

重音：重音在这个词的重读元音上。

5. Darren usually leaves me notes, sticky notes with instructions.发音：Darren 两个音都读得非常圆润；usually leaves me notes 很有节奏感。

重音与弱读：sticky 在这个元音上加重音；with 是机不可闻的介词，with 和后面的instructions 连在一起，再带一点点语调。

6. Never mind, the menu's changed.语调：never mind 主要是在音调上比较特别；the menu's changed 用同样的音调变化方式。

7. I'm having scrambled eggs.重音：把重音放在scrambled 的开头音节上。

8. Good. Is that enough? That's perfect.语调：Good 音调稍微往上一点。

连读与吞音：is that 中间有连读，把that 的t 吞掉；that's（perfect 前）依然机不可闻，读得越轻越好；perfect 把per 加重音。

词汇与注释：omelet 欧姆蛋（煎蛋卷）；scrambled eggs 炒蛋；sticky notes 便利贴；instructions 说明指示；menu's changed 菜单变了。本段大量极轻虚词（got、it's、with、that's），要读到几乎听不见。

第10 讲探险致辞选段：新增航程、南太平洋与南极（add another leg）原文台词：Thank you for doing the tour. Stepping into my shoes, it is important. Actually, now I've made the decision, I'm rather looking forward to it. And we've managed to add another leg to the journey, the South Pacific and the Antarctic. — Boys will be boys. — Excuse me,subzero temperatures, men will be men.逐句精读：

1. Thank you for doing the tour.发音：thank you 好像从喉咙里挤出来，用气息发声。tour 这个音发得非常深——不是纯粹按音标发，而是更深、更矜持的读法。

2. it is important.连读：it is 连读。

重音：important 重音在o 上，并且把整个发音往后放、再往后放。

3. Actually, now I've made the decision,语调：now 往上升；读到音快结束时直接把I 的音发出来。

弱读与吞音：I've 非常弱，只是把牙齿放在下唇上做了个v 的动作但没发出来；made 的d 不发音。

4. I'm rather looking forward to it.重音：把这一句里的三个重读元音突出出来。

5. And we've managed to add another leg to the journey重音：把we've 和add 这两个重读元音，一个往上、一个往下突出；managed to的t 吞掉。

重音与吞音：another leg 两个元音重读；leg 的g 吞掉；to 弱化快速读过去，仍读成弱音。

6. the South Pacific and the Antarctic.发音：Pacific 这个音拐弯拐得有点多；Antarctic 同样。

7. Boys will be boys. Men will be men.语调：注意两组will be 的声调对比，Boys、Men 各带一组音调。

8. 整段感受（老师收束提醒）整篇读下来很累，因为要一直提着气去读，并且要把音调完全沉下去。定好沉下去的基调，是这一类贵族腔演讲的关键。

词汇与注释：do the tour 做这次导览；step into my shoes 站到我这个位置；look forward to 期待；add another leg to the journey 给旅程再加一段；South Pacific 南太平洋；Antarctic 南极；subzero temperatures 零下的温度。

第11 讲南方与北方选段：向伊莎贝尔表白（freely and proudly）原文台词：I state freely and proudly, Isabel, that I've fallen in love with you, and I want to spend what remains of my life in your company. I believe I could make you happy. — At any rate, I should very much like the chance to try. — Well, goodness, Lord Merton! I freely admit you've taken me by surprise.逐句精读：

1. I state freely and proudly, Isabel,吞音：freely、and、proudly 三个词的结尾音都吞掉。

语调：Isabel 往上升。

2. that I've fallen in love with you,吞音与连读：that 吞掉；that I've 前面的have 不发音，直接缩成I've，三个音非常紧凑地连在一起；fallen in 两个直接连读。

发音：love 的v 轻轻读——把牙齿抵在下唇上发一个很轻的v，发完直接把with带出来。

3. and I want to spend what remains of my life in your company.连读与吞音：and I 连在一起；want to 中间吞掉一个t，to 弱读加快读的速度。

吞音：spend 的d 吞掉；what 吞掉；remains of 连读，of 读得轻一点快一点。

语调与对比：life 轻微往上升；in your 对比明显——in 是虚词、没有实际含义，轻读；you 要强调、加重音。

4. I believe I could make you happy.吞音：只需要简单吞掉could 的d。

5. At any rate, I should very much like the chance to try.连读与吞音：at any 连读；should 的d 吞掉；very 加重音。

发音：like 的k 不发出声音，但要做出发音的动作，中间停顿一小下；chance to try 的to 发成轻t、快速带过。

6. 整段感受（老师收束提醒）整段之所以读得顺畅，是因为中间用了非常多的省略和连读；把省略与连读吃透，发音会有比较大的提升。

词汇与注释：state 郑重地说；freely and proudly 坦荡而自豪地；fall in love with 爱上；in your company 在你身边；At any rate 无论如何；taken me by surprise 使我猝不及防。

第12 讲唐顿庄园选段：品味是迈克尔的、要闯自己的路（strike out）原文台词：... most sophisticated room I've ever been in. The taste is all Michael's. He was very artistic and very clever. — So you're going to live here? — I think I'll live here more. I'd like a life away from Downton. — Because you like London? — Well, the house, the estate, they're all Mary's now, more than Papa realizes. It's time for me to strike out in my own direction, not just dawdle in Mary's wake.逐句精读：

1. The taste is all Michael's.连读：两处连读——taste 和is 之间、is 和all 之间。

重音：注意整句的重音所在与语调起伏。

2. He was very artistic and very clever.重音：very 加重音（两处very 都加重）。

词汇：artistic 形容人时指有艺术天赋、有艺术品味，也可引申为有创造力、有美感。

发音与弱读：clever 读法很有特色，有一种独有的英文风范，稍微往上升一点点；连接两个形容词的and 仅仅发了一个/n/ 的音，快速带过。

3. I think I'll live here more.连读：think I'll 两个连在一起。

语调：think I'll 先升再降；结尾more 是唐顿里常见的结尾音读法——快速的升降变化。

4. I'd like a life away from Downton.连读：只有一处，like a 连读。

节奏：最要注意的还是它的语调和重音所在，整句节奏感非常强。

5. the house, the estate, they're all Mary's now,语调：前面两个名词the house、the estate 都采取了声调的读法。

词汇与重音：estate 是庄园的意思，英式历史剧常见用法，尤其指贵族家族传承的大片产业；Mary 加重音，强调庄园现在已属大小姐；最后一个now 拐弯读出来。

6. more than Papa realizes.语调：结尾realizes 做了一个小小的转弯式读法（贵族结尾音）。

7. It's time for me to strike out in my own direction,重音与连读：time 加重音；两处连读，strike 与out、out 与in；最后在strike 这里再加一个重音。

词汇与发音：strike out 在拳击里是“出击”，这里是比喻，显示他要独立开创、闯出自己的路；这个词尾音鼻音特别重，鼻子偏上方有震动时就找对了位置。

8. not just dawdle in Mary's wake.发音：not just 里the u 音读得非常深；dawdle 后面wa 音读得比较长。

词汇：dawdle in someone's wake 是固定用法，比喻跟在某人后面慢吞吞地混日子、没有自己的追求。

词汇与注释：sophisticated 精致考究的；the taste is all Michael's 这份品味全是迈克尔的；estate 庄园；strike out 出击、闯新路；dawdle in one's wake 跟随其后混日子。

第13 讲唐顿庄园选段：两位女士独处伦敦、我开过救护车（confidence in my abilities）原文台词：Isn't this exciting? Two ladies alone in London. Whatever shall we do? — You have to take me straight to the doctor. — Delighted. So thrilling — the talking cure is quite the thing, you know. — Perhaps don't mention to your father that I brought the motor. He seems to have very little confidence in my abilities, even though I managed to drive an ambulance through the warzone in another country, no less.逐句精读：

1. Isn't this exciting?吞音：两处吞音，isn't 的t、exciting 的t。

2. Two ladies alone in London.连读：两处连读，ladies alone 连、alone in 连。

节奏：重音放在ladies、alone、London 三个词上；中间的the 读轻。

3. Whatever shall we do?重音：只有两个重音，what 和do；最后的do 把音调再往下沉一点。

4. What adventures lie in wait?连读：what 和adventure 之间连在一起。

语调：adventures 先升再降；最后的wait 先降再升，但比较轻微。

5. Perhaps don't mention to your father that I brought the motor.吞音：两处，don't 的t、brought 的t。

连读：that 和I 之间连读。

语调：掌握整句结构与重音；最后的motor 先升再降，是贵族发音里很有特点的结尾方式。

6. He seems to have very little confidence in my abilities.弱读与连读：have 的h 不发音（have very 读成a very）；confidence 和in 之间连读。

重音：重音只放在三个词上——he（强调人物）、confidence、abilities（最后一个词）；abilities 除了重音还要加音调，同样是轻轻地、缓缓地加上去。

词汇与注释：the talking cure 谈话疗法（指看心理医生）；quite the thing 很时髦、很流行；the motor 汽车；have confidence in one's abilities 对某人的能力有信心；warzone 战区；no less 更别说、更何况。

第14 讲傲慢与偏见选段：拒婚对峙（in trade, reduced circumstances）原文台词：... I deserve to know why. — It offends me that you should speak to me as if it were your duty to rescue my reputation. I think that because you are rich and my father is in reduced circumstances, that you can have me for your possession. I suppose I should expect no less from someone in trade. — I don't want to possess you. I wish to marry you because I love you. — I do not like you, and never have.逐句精读：

1. It offends me that you should speak to me连读与重音：it a 之间连读；offends 加重音。

词汇：offend 是冒犯、情感上的触怒，比anger、upset 更正式、更文雅，强调对尊严和体面的伤害。

吞音与弱读：that you 直接连；should 的d 吞掉、speak 的k 吞掉；should 在这里不是“应该”，而是“竟然”，表达惊讶；因为太生气，这句甚至有一点破音感。

2. as if it were your duty to rescue my reputation.吞音与连读：as if 连读、if it 连读、it 的t 吞掉，三个词读起来只剩as-if-it 几个音；duty 这个词像咬着牙在读，压着怒气。

语法与情感：这句用了标准的虚拟语气（as if it were...），在礼貌的措辞下是致命的攻击，既体现语言能力也体现角色教养，很有艺术性。

3. You think that because you are rich要点：用气声的方式说；两处吞音——think 的k、that 的t；重音在think 和rich两个词上。

4. and my father is in reduced circumstances,吞音与连读：and 的d 吞掉；my 加重音。

词汇：reduced circumstances 指家境败落、生活拮据，是经典又委婉的表达。

5. that you can have me for your possession.吞音与节奏：that 吞掉；后面几个词注意升降升降的节奏感。

6. I suppose I should expect no less from someone in trade.重音与连读：I 加重音；should expect 之间连读，expect 的t 吞掉。

语调：no less 降升降；someone in 之间连读；in trade 最后一个音降升。

7. I do not like you, and never have.要点：这句已经很生气；not 后面的to（此处to 弱读）吞掉；读得干脆、利落。

词汇与注释：offend 冒犯、触怒；as if it were your duty 仿佛那是你的义务；rescue my reputation替我挽回名声；reduced circumstances 家道中落；possession 私有物；someone in trade 做买卖的人（当时带轻视）。

第15 讲权贵施压选段：书信、家族名义、绞刑（family name, hang him）原文台词：Here are letters supporting your case, to everyone from the Royal College of Surgeons to the Prime Minister. Not yet. Prepare your examinations for the next two years, and keep your word not to see him. And I will send these backed by the family name. And if I do see him, then the law will hang him, and I'll burn these letters. You'll never be a doctor.逐句精读：

1. Here are letters supporting your case语调：波浪形的读法，先定住调子。

连读：here all（here are）用here 结尾的r 音连着后面一起读；后面的supporting your case 也是波浪形。

2. Prepare your examinations语调：到examination 这里拐了个弯——音标重音在na-tion，但读时读成na-tions（抑扬顿挫）。

3. for the next two years,弱读：for the 两个非常轻地带过去，for 弱读；next two 连读时吞音。

重音与语调：two 加重音强调时间；years 先降再升。

4. and keep your word not to see him.吞音：and 的d 吞掉、word 的d 吞掉、not 的t 吞掉。

重音：see 加重音。

5. And I will send these backed by the family name.发音：这里的and 的d 没有吞掉，而是舌头在这个音上放一下、直接滑到下一个音。

重音与情感：I 加重音；send 的d 吞掉；these 加重音；family name 后面两个音隐隐有一种炫耀、骄傲的感觉。

6. And if I do see him,连读与重音：and 的d 完全吞掉；if I 连读；do 加重音强调。

7. then the law will hang him,重音：then the 两个重音。

语调与连读：hang him 是“噔噔”降声；两处连读——him 和and 连、and 吞d 之后的n 和I 连。

8. and I'll burn these letters.语调：letters 升降；You'll never be a doctor 里never 加重音。

词汇与注释：letters supporting your case 为你辩白的信件；Royal College of Surgeons 皇家外科医学院；Prime Minister 首相；examinations 考试；keep your word 守信用；backed by the family name 以家族名义作保；hang him 绞死他。

第16 讲傲慢与偏见选段：自尊是缺点还是美德（pride a fault or a virtue）原文台词：You're too proud, Mr. Darcy. — And would you consider pride a fault or a virtue? — That I couldn't say, because we're doing our best to find fault in you. — Maybe it's that I find it hard to forgive the follies and vices of others, or their offences against me. My good opinion once lost is lost forever. — My dear, I cannot tease you about that. What a shame,for I dearly love to laugh. A family trait, I think.逐句精读：

1. You're too proud, Mr. Darcy.重音：proud 加重音。

语调：Mr. Darcy 先降再升。

2. would you consider pride a fault or a virtue?吞音与语调：and 的d 吞掉；后面三个词音调偏高，试着升八度。would you 之间连读。

重音与连读：重音在pride、fault、virtue 三个词；pride 和a 连读；fault 和or 之间不直接连读，但嘴型从/ɔ/ 连贯地变到/ə/；a virtue 很快。

3. because we're doing our best to find fault in you.重音：真正的重音只有fault 一个，其余词是为凸显节奏感——doing、best、find比our、to 稍重。

方法：先把节奏框架定住，再加发音技巧；find 和a 连读、fault 和in 连读，最后把整句串起来。

4. My dear, I cannot tease you about that.连读与语调：dear 和I 连在一起，还要加一个先降再升的调；cannot 的t 吞掉；tease you 连读。

吞音与语调：about that 里about 的t 吞掉；最后的that 把调子往上升。

5. What a shame, for I dearly love to laugh.连读与重音：what a 连读；shame 加一点重音。

弱读：for 发的是弱读音；后面四个词节奏感比较明显。

词汇与注释：proud 骄傲；fault 缺点；virtue 美德；find fault in you 挑你的错；folly and vice 愚蠢与恶习；offence 冒犯；tease 取笑；What a shame 多可惜；a family trait 一个家族特质。

第17 讲哈利·波特选段：屋顶、隆巴顿（cockney, roof）原文台词：How about the roof? — What's the matter, Neville? Bit beyond your reach! — Harry, no!You heard what Madam Hooch said. Besides, you don't even know how to fly! — Give it here, Longbottom, or I'll lock you in your room! — Is that so? Have it your way, then.逐句精读：

1. I think I'll leave it somewhere.连读：think 和I 连读。

吞音：leave it 的t 吞掉，同时和前面的leave 连读在一起。

口音与语调：人名Longbottom 原本读long-BOT-tom，这里采用伦敦腔（cockney）标志性的吞t，后面不再读成button 而是Boom-t'm；think 加重音，中间几个词调子比较平，最后先升再降再升、拐一个弯。

2. How about the roof?发音：how about 不算连读但很连贯——how 发完/aʊ/ 直接把嘴型放到下一个音的开头；about the 的t、the 两个音也没连读，但转换非常快，舌头在发t 的同时快速往the 上转，且t 不能发得太清晰。

语调：roof 先升再降。

3. What's the matter, Neville?节奏：主要特征是整句的节奏感，重音放在三个词上；中间的the 读轻。

4. Bit beyond your reach!吞音：bit 的t 吞掉；最后两个音拐弯比较多。

5. Is that so? Have it your way, then.吞音与连读：that 的t 吞掉；have it 连读、把it 的t 吞掉。

语调：have it 先降再升；最后your way then 把最后一个音调升上去。

词汇与注释：How about the roof 屋顶上怎么样；What's the matter 怎么了；Neville Longbottom 纳威·隆巴顿；beyond your reach 你够不着；Madam Hooch 霍琦夫人；lock you in your room 把你关进房间；Have it your way 随你的便。

第18 讲舞会邀请选段：能不能带上我和奥利弗（invitation, beg）原文台词：Is it an invitation? What are you doing there? Are you invited to dinner? — No, to a reception after dinner. Although I dare say there'll be something to eat later on. — But I'm not sure we'll go. — Not go? We barely know her. And it's hard to get up much enthusiasm for something that begins so late in the evening. — Well, but surely... — What is it you're trying to ask? — I just hoped that Oliver and I might be included in the invitation, but you're not. Please don't make me beg, Mother. Would it be so terribly difficult for you to write and ask?逐句精读：

1. Is it an invitation?连读：两处连读——is 和it（z 连）、it 和an（t 连）。

2. Are you invited to dinner?吞音：invited 结尾轻辅音t 吞掉，直接接后面的to，把to 发成轻t。

重音：dinner 最后加一点重音。

3. Not go?要点：这两个词故意放慢语速，所以第一个not 的t 并没有吞掉；go 试着拐好几个弯上去。

4. Well, but surely...吞音与重音：but 的t 吞掉；surely 加重音。

语调：you 再往上提一点，因为本来想往下说、却被对方打断了。

5. I just hoped that Oliver and I might be included in the invitation.吞音：hoped 中间的t 失去爆破吞掉。

连读：后面四个词每两个之间都有技巧——that 和Oliver 连读；Oliver 和and 连读（r 与an 连）；and 的d 几乎吞掉，舌位放一下直接滑到后面的I。might 的t吞掉；included 中间升一下；in the invitation 比较平稳。

6. Please don't make me beg, Mother.语调：注意三个音调——please 往上升、beg 往下降、Mother 收尾。

7. Would it be so terribly difficult for you to write and ask?语调与连读：would 先把音调提上去并保持两个词；would 和it 连读，it 的t 也吞掉。

重音与弱读：terribly 加重音；for 非常轻，读成/fə/ 带过去。

连读：and 和ask 连读。

词汇与注释：invitation 邀请；reception 招待会；I dare say 我敢说；get up enthusiasm for 对……提起兴致；be included in the invitation 被列入邀请名单；beg 乞求；so terribly difficult真有那么难吗。

第19 讲内阁博弈选段：在沙地上划一条线（cabinet, draw a line）原文台词：He was a little surprised, I think. — Really? — I dare not express any view at all. — Papa was always patting his hands. I think one should start; one means to continue. —Absolutely. Put a marker down. Quite. Draw a line in the sand. — He said he'd put it to cabinet, and I said there was nothing to discuss. — Good for you.逐句精读：

1. He was a little surprised, I think.连读与弱读：was 和a 连在一起；little surprised 很轻很轻地带过去，把嘴型放好后尽量用气声带过去、而不是喉咙声；surprised 后面的音连在一起。

语调：I think 先降再升。

2. I dare not express any view at all.连读：dare 和express 连读（用dare 的尾音连）；express 和any 也连读在一起。

发音与语调：view at all 里at 弱读、a 连读；把o 音发得深一点，感觉胸腔都在震动；音调稍微拐一个弯，但不明显、只轻轻带一点上去。

3. Papa was always patting his hands.语调与连读：Papa 稍微往上升一点；was always 连读。

重音与鼻音：patting 加重音；后面的in（his 的尾音处理）直接放一个鼻音上去，不要发得那么清楚。

4. I think one should start; one means to continue.吞音与重音：think 可以吞掉，用来凸显后面的one；should 的do（d）吞掉；start 加重音、和前面的音连在一起。

语调：continue 降升降。

5. Draw a line in the sand.语调：整句语调最起初往上升，一直到最后整个调子再降下来。

连读与鼻音：line 和后面的in 连读；in 的读法和上面一样，放鼻音、别发那么清楚。

6. He said he'd put it to cabinet,连读与吞音：put 和it 连读、it 的t 吞掉；to 发轻一点。

语调：cabinet 先降再升。

7. and I said there was nothing to discuss.连读与吞音：后面读得非常快——the 吞掉，an（and）和I 直接滑在一起。

重音：后半句把重音放在最后一个词discuss 上。

词汇与注释：express any view 发表任何意见；pat one's hands 拍手；put a marker down 立个标杆；draw a line in the sand 划清界限；put it to cabinet 提交内阁讨论；nothing to discuss 没什么可商量的。

第20 讲加冕王后选段：从塔里放出表亲（coronation, tower）原文台词：You sent for me? — Yes. I am not yet recovered from my childbirth. You are thankful for the coronation, that I shall take you as my queen. — I would be thankful if you freed my cousin from the tower and let my mother be with me. I have said why I cannot, and you cannot ask me to be thankful. — What will be my role when I am queen, and what will I do for you?逐句精读：

1. You sent for me?吞音与弱读：sent 的t 吞掉；把后面的for 弱读成/fə/，快速带过去。

2. I am not yet recovered from my childbirth.重音：I 把音加重一些；not yet 先降再升，别太明显、稍给一点即可。

发音：recovered from my childbirth 后面平稳读下去即可；词尾d 轻吞。

3. I would be thankful if you freed my cousin from the tower重音：would 加重音，而且它的尾音没有吞——加了重音之后要把全部音发出来，更能凸显；后面的音拉得比较平。

重音与弱读：freed my cousin 两个词加重音；from 读快一点、轻一点。

语调：tower 是升降声，但依旧给得不要太多，一点点就好。

4. and let my mother be with me.重音：let 加重音；me 虽被稍微压了一点，但音是有一点点往上的。

5. What will be my role when I am queen?语调：注意What will be my role 的语调。

连读：when 和I 连在一起（when I）。

6. and what will I do for you?吞音与连读：and 的d 吞掉；will 和I 连在一起读。

弱读：for 依然读成弱音/fə/。

词汇与注释：send for me 派人来找我；recovered from my childbirth 产后尚未恢复；coronation 加冕礼；take you as my queen 立你为王后；freed my cousin from the tower 把我的表亲从塔里放出；role 角色、职责。

第21 讲家书絮语选段：独女的位置（solitary daughter, bear it）原文台词：It is a delight to see my Cassie in such high spirits. She has waited one whole year for her beloved to come home. — Who are you writing to, Eliza? — She must be as excited to see Tom as we are. — I have such butterflies. — I will admit to some butterflies of my own,Eliza, for it is at this point Cassie and I must separate, and I will take up the position of solitary daughter. I cannot welcome it, but I suppose I must bear it.逐句精读：

1. It is a delight to see my Cassie in such high spirits.连读：前面it is、is a 都有连读。

语调：delight 把音往上提一点；to see 不是硬碰的to，而是顺势滑连。

重音与连读：Cassie 把音加重一点；in 跟着Cassie 的尾音顺势滑下来，想象成一条平稳的弧线；such 加重音；spirits 先升再降。

2. She has waited one whole year for her beloved to come home.重音：she、waited 各加一点重音；one whole year 三个词读清楚，用来强调时间的长度。

弱读与连读：for 弱读（不读o、读成弱音）；her 跟前面用一条圆滑的弧线接过来，接得圆润；come home 两个音滑着连在一起，尾音很有韵味。

3. I will admit to some butterflies of my own,连读：will 的结尾l 和admit 的元音连在一起。

弱读：to 弱读；own 这个词也很有特色。

4. for it is at this point Cassie and I must separate,连读：前面有好几处连读——for it、it is、this at；at 的t 吞掉，连起来像for it is at this point。

重音与吞音：point 这个词要重读；Cassie and 先把d 吞掉再和后面的I 连；must的t 吞掉；separate 加一点重音。

5. and I will take up the position of solitary daughter.连读：and I 连在一起；take up 连读；position 和of 连读。

6. I cannot welcome it, but I suppose I must bear it.语调：welcome it 是升降声；but I 连在一起；suppose 先降再升。

吞音与连读：must 的t 吞掉；bear it 先降再升，并把两个音连在一起。

词汇与注释：in high spirits 兴致高涨；beloved 心爱的人；come home 回家；butterflies（in one's stomach）心里发慌、紧张；separate 分开；take up the position of solitary daughter 当起“独女”的角色；bear it 忍受、承受。
