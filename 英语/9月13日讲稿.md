《小王子》第12-13 课、第21 课听课笔记

第一部分第12-13 课

老师开场欢迎同学们回到小王子第十二课。第十一课中小王子来到了地球，这一课他继续旅行，探索不一样的世界。本课词汇量不大，大概学习三十个高频基础词汇。还会讲解几个关键的希腊神话故事，帮助大家记忆词汇。学习四个句法，其中同位语是之前没学过的，会重点讲。练习断句方法，讲解小王子创作的关键时代背景。最后带大家复述故事，开展主题讨论。

原文片段：The little prince crossed the desert and only met a flower, a very ordinary flower with three petals.翻译：小王子穿越了沙漠，只遇到了一朵花，一个非常寻常的花，有着三个花瓣。词汇讲解：ordinary = a very common flower，非常常见、很普通的。可以替换成common。反义词是extraordinary（奇特的、不同寻常的），把extra 拿掉就是ordinary（寻常的）。petals 是花瓣的意思，之前讲过。原文片段：The little prince was written by a pilot, a famous science fiction writer.翻译：小王子这本书是由一个飞行员写的，一个著名的科幻作家。语法讲解——同位语：

同位语就是对前面出现的名词或者代词进行一个信息的补充。比如"a flower, a very ordinary flower with three petals"，框框里面的部分用来解释什么样的flower。再比如"The little prince was written by a pilot, a famous science fiction writer"，先说小王子是由一个飞行员写出来的，然后用同位语"a famous science fiction writer"进一步解释是什么样的飞行员。两个身份用同位语拼接起来。

原文片段："Good morning," said the little prince. "Good morning," said the flower. "Where are all the people?" asked the little prince politely. "The flower had once seen a caravan go by."翻译：早上好呀，小王子说。早上好，花儿说道。人们都去哪儿了呀，小王子很礼貌的问道。花儿曾经见过一个商队过去。

词汇讲解：caravan 指沙漠里的骆驼商队，也可以指车队、一大个车队。"The flower had once seen a caravan go by"中had once seen 表示曾经有一次看见。

原文片段："People?There are six or seven of them, I think. I saw them once years ago, but you can never find them. They're blown by the wind. They haven't got any roots, you see. That's their problem."翻译：人们？大概有六七个吧，我认为。我看见过他们一次，很多年以前，但你永远都找不到他们。他们被风吹走了。他们没有任何的根系，你看。那就是他们的问题。

词汇讲解：years ago 表示多年以前，once 表示一次。blow 表示吹（气/风），blow blow blow the winter wind。被动语态"they're blown by the wind"强调这些人被风给吹走了。roots 表示根、树根，"they haven't got any roots"表示他们没有任何根，漂泊不定的状态，没有根的。

这描述的现象可能跟小王子作者安托万的经历有关系。作为一个飞行员，没日没夜地在天空当中飞，一年都回不了家几次，所以这种漂泊不定的感觉贯穿了安托万的人生。同时也反映了当时的一个时代背景——社会学家齐格蒙特·鲍曼提出了“流动的现代性”，他认为现代的人是没有根的，人们都迁徙到城市里面了，也没有固定的家乡，到处像候鸟一样迁徙，人与人之间的关系非常浅、非常脆弱，产生了一种身份认同的危机，就是没有归属感。

原文片段：The little prince climbed a high mountain. The only mountains he'd ever known had been his three volcanoes, which only came up to his knees. He used to use the extinct volcano as a stool.翻译：小王子爬上了一座高高的山。他所知道的山只有三座火山，到他膝盖那么高。他曾经使用那座死火山把它当做凳子来坐。

词汇讲解：volcanoes 是火山的意思。v 往往代表山谷，是一种象形。volcano 跟山有关。词源来自希腊神话中铁匠之神Vulcan，Vulcan 是打铁、烧火的神，充满了创造力，演化出来了volcano 这个词。extinct 表示出去了、离开了、彻底灭绝了、消失了（x前缀表示出去，e 表示往外推），extinct volcano 就是消失的火山、死火山、灭绝的火山。stool 就是那种小板凳。

语法讲解——过去完成时：这句话中用到了过去完成时。定语从句中"he had ever known"是过去完成时，"had been his three volcanoes"也是过去完成时。为什么要用过去完成时？画时间线来看：he climbed a high mountain 是在过去的某个时间点；在此之前（过去的过去）he'd ever known（他所知道的），也就是在绿色的时间段当中，他只知道这个世界上有三座火山，直到他爬上了现在的这座高山，他才意识到世界上的山原来不只有他们家的火山，还有各种各样很壮丽的山。两个时间对比出来，就理解了过去完成时的原理。

So he thought to himself, "I'll be able to see the whole planet and all the people at a single glance from the top of a high mountain like this." But all he could see were sharp spiky rocks.翻译：于是他又开始自己思考，我能够看到整个星球和所有的人，从这个高山的顶上一眼就能看到所有的人和整个星球。但他所能看到的只有尖锐的、嶙峋的岩石。

词汇讲解：single 表示单个的一个，也就是one。某人单身会说he's single/she's single。光棍节叫Singles Day。glance 表示瞥一眼。at a single glance 就是用一眼。sharp 表示尖锐的，之前学过sharpen his pencil（削铅笔），动词sharpen 表示削尖，所以sharp 表示尖锐的。spiky 表示像棍子一样的、很扎人的、嶙峋的、有刺的。spike 名词表示长棍，古代有一种刑罚叫put someone's head on the spike（把某人的头砍下来放在棍子上面）。on the off chance 表示抱有一点点希望（chance 表示机会，off 表示远离，偏离的机会=希望不太大但仍然抱有一丝希望）。希腊神话故事——Echo 与回声：小王子不抱太多希望地说hello，然后一个回声回应他。echo 这个词来自于古希腊小仙女的名字Echo。故事是这样的：宙斯特别花心，喜欢跟很多小仙女约会。Echo 特别喜欢多管闲事，有一次宙斯和其他仙女约会的时候，Echo 帮他隐瞒了赫拉来的时候，缠住赫拉不让她抓到宙斯。赫拉知道后非常生气，就给Echo 下了诅咒：你以后再也不能开口说话了，你只能重复别人说的话，变成鹦鹉学舌的状态。从此Echo 就不会说话了，只会重复他听到的声音。直到他遇到了心爱的美少年，美少年问“你是谁呀？"(Who are you?) Echo 只能回复"Who are you?"，美少年说”你为什么在重复我说的话？"(Why are you repeating my words?) Echo 又说"Why are you repeating my words?"，美少年就恼火了。就这样Echo 再也没有追到他心爱的人，心碎了一地。所以从希腊神话演变过来，echo 就变成了回声的意思，因为它只会重复别人说的话。

词汇讲解：stretch 作为动词表示伸展身体(stretch his body)，作为名词表示伸展的一长条的东西，也就是一段。long stretches of sand, rocks and snow 表示很长的一段沙漠、石头和雪地，表示这种地形。也可以说a long stretch of grassland（一段很长的草地）。discover 表示发现，名词形式是discovery。

原文片段："Then he said to himself, 'I thought I was rich with a unique flower, when all I own is a common rose and three volcanoes that only come up to my knees, one of which may be extinct forever. Anyway it doesn't make me a very great prince.'"翻译：然后他又开始自己思考了，我以为我很富有，有着一朵独一无二的玫瑰花，当我所有拥有的东西就是一朵普通的玫瑰以及三座火山，这些火山只有我膝盖那么高，其中的一座还可能已经永远地灭绝掉了。不管怎样这并没有使我成为一个非常好的王子。

词汇讲解：unique 表示独一无二的，也可以说special（特别的）。common rose 表示普通的玫瑰，前面学过ordinary，这是一堆近义词（很普通、很寻常的）。forever 表示永远。长句断句练习：I thought I was rich with a unique flower, when all I own is a common rose and three volcanoes that only come up to my knees, one of which may be extinct forever. Anyway it doesn't make me a very great prince.主干：I thought I was rich. 当(when)我所有拥有的东西就是一朵普通的玫瑰和三座火山。什么样的火山？直到我膝盖那么高的火山。其中一座可能已经永远地死去了（是死火山）。整句话结构层层递进，最重要的部分在这里：I thought I was rich with a unique flower（我以为我有着一朵花就非常富有了），而实际上是什么样的呢？后面用一个状语连接一下，当我所拥有的就是一朵花和三个火山。然后再形容一下是什么样的火山，整句话就完成了。

翻译：他躺在了草地当中，哭了。词汇讲解：lay down 表示躺下来。动词原型是lie，lie 表示躺下来。之前也学过lie 表示撒谎的意思，所以要根据语境，一个单词可能有多个意思。创作背景讲解：安托万创作这一段文字的背景：他的妻子其实就是那一朵独特的玫瑰。而那五千多朵其他的长得很相似的花呢，可能就代表了他之前背叛妻子所接触的这些女性。在接触了这些女性之后，他才意识到其实他最喜欢的还是他的老婆。所以这一段可能反映出了他对爱、自由和家庭责任之间的内心矛盾和挣扎。玫瑰花经常会弄一些情感上的戏剧性的事情从而博取小王子的关注，这在心理学上可能就是一种焦虑型的心理或性格，小的时候有一些分离焦虑，所以在成年过程当中，如果别人离开了或者不满足自己的要求，可能会无比的焦虑。she's really dramatic（她非常具有戏剧性）。

看图复述故事，把句子填写完整：The little prince crossed the desert and met a flower. The flower told him that people were hard to find, because they were blown by the wind and had no roots. Next he climbed a mountain hoping to see the whole planet and all the people, but he only found spiky rocks, and the only response he got was an echo. Finally he discovered a rose garden and realized that his flower was not unique. He saw thousands of roses that looked exactly the same as his rose, and this made him very sad, because he had always thought that his flower was one of a kind in the whole universe.

话题一：孤独讨论问题：Why do you think people feel lonely even when they are surrounded by others?（为什么你认为人们会感到孤独即使当他们被很多人环绕着？）
<!--col:R-->


<!--/folio-->

<!--folio:第9页-->

<!--col:L-->

<!--col:M-->

参考句型：I think people feel lonely because even when we are with others, loneliness can happen if... In my opinion, true connection comes from...老师给出的参考回答：I think people feel lonely because they don't feel valued or truly connected with others, even if they're surrounded by people. If there's no deep understanding or emotional bond, they might still feel lonely. For example, attending a large party where no one really listens to you can make someone feel lonelier than being alone. Therefore, true connection comes from meaningful conversations and shared interests where people are willing to support and understand each other.词汇：be surrounded by（被...环绕）、valued（被重视）、seen（被看见）、connected（有连接感）、emotional bond（情感纽带）、isolated（被孤立的/像孤岛一样的）、meaningful conversations（有意义的对话）、shared interests（共同的兴趣）。话题二：独特性与价值讨论问题：The little prince believed his flower was unique. How do you define the value of something or someone in your life?（小王子相信他的花是独特的，你如何定义某件事物或者某个人在你生命当中的价值？）参考句型：To me, the value of something comes from... I think something becomes valuable when... In my opinion, what makes someone or something special is...老师给出的参考回答：To me, the value of someone or something comes from the bond we share with them. For example, an old book given by a loved one may hold more value than a brand new book, because of the memories and feelings attached to it. This means that something becomes valuable when it becomes a part of our life.

词汇：bond（纽带）、emotional bond（情感纽带）、loved one（心爱的人/家人）、attached to（附带的/承载的）、memories（回忆）、feelings（感受）、valuable（有价值的）、meaningful（有意义的）。课程总结：这堂课词汇量不大，大概30 来个高频基础词汇。讲了两个重要的希腊神话故事：一个是Vulcan（火山之神的来源，volcano 由此演化），还有Echo（回声和小仙女的故事）。学习了四个句法，其中的同位语是这堂课刚接触的，要重点复习。用了断句的方法帮助大家理解长句中的意群，把句子拆分成有意义的语块再组合起来理解。了解了小王子创作玫瑰花园的一些背景和他妻子之间的爱恨纠葛，也了解了现代人缺乏归属感的现代性感受。最后复述了故事，讨论了两个话题：孤独和独特的价值。

第二部分第21 课

老师开场：上一堂课我们的小王子遇到了地球上的5000 朵玫瑰，这一次小王子会去哪儿呢？又会遇见谁呢？我们的狐狸即将出场。本课要学习40 个高频基础词汇，在此基础上展开讲常见的象形词源和拉丁词根。还会讲几个比较难的句法，大部分在之前课程中已经遇到过了，其他没遇到过的会重点展开讲。会了解一些文化背景知识，学习一个重要的阅读方法——分段复述法（读了前面不忘后面，用自己的话来总结故事）。最重要的是学习整个小王子书籍当中最经典的金句，里面有六个情感和哲学方面的表达句型，可以活学活用用到自己的人生感悟抒发上。然后复述故事。

词汇讲解：responsible 表示负责的，responsible for something 表示对什么东西负责。you become responsible for those you tame（你变得对那些你驯化的东西负责）。forever是副词表示永远。truth 可以指真理也可以指真相（个体化的truth=你自己的真理，universal truth=普世真理）。责任与承诺：关于爱和责任，小王子对他付出过的要负责任，承担自己的义务。这种责任感在英文中也可以说是commitment（承诺）。make a commitment to something 表示对什么东西做出承诺/做出奉献和承诺，这种承诺不一定指人与人之间的关系/情感关系，也可以是工作、事业、个人成长、项目等。make a commitment to a project/make a commitment to your career。责任感也可以说a sense of responsibility。

情感类句型一：原句：My life's boring, but if you tame me, my life will become sunny.句型：My life is ___, but if ___, my life will ___.仿写示例：My life is ordinary, but if I travel, my life will become exciting.（我的生活可能很普通，但如果我去旅行，我的生活就充满了激情。）仿写示例：My life is stressful, but if I find a hobby, my life will feel better.（我的生活可能压力很大，但如果我找到一个爱好，我的人生会感觉起来好一点。）情感类句型二：原句：You are beautiful, but empty.句型：Something is ___, but ___.（用一个连词来连接两个相反的特质）仿写示例：The book is interesting, but challenging.（这本书很有意思，但是也具有挑战性。）哲理句型一：原句：We only really see with our hearts.句型：We only + 副词+ 动词+ with + 某样事物.仿写示例：We only truly understand with experience.（只有用亲身经历才能够真正理解。）仿写示例：We only really learn with deliberate practice.（我们只有多多刻意练习才能够学得好。）哲理句型二：原句：What matters is invisible to the eyes.句型：What matters is + 名词/形容词.仿写示例：What matters is kindness.（重要的是善良/与人为善。）仿写示例：What matters is love.（重要的是爱。）

请大家回想一下小王子和狐狸的相遇，以及他们之间的对话，用以下六个问题来复述：

1. Who did the little prince meet and what did he ask? The little prince met a fox. He asked the fox to play with him because he was feeling sad.

2. Why couldn't the fox play with the little prince? What does tamed mean? The fox said he couldn't play with him because he hadn't been tamed. He explained that tamed means making ties and needing each other.

3. What would happen if the little prince tamed the fox? The fox said his life would become sunny and full of meaning. He would find joy in the little prince's footsteps, and the golden cornfields would remind him of the little prince's hair.

4. What did the little prince learn about people and friendship from the fox? The fox said that people haven't got time to know about things anymore because they buy ready-made things in shops, but friendship can't be bought. He told the little prince that taming (or knowing) someone requires patience.

5. What did the fox teach the little prince about his rose? The fox told the little prince to see the roses again and compare them with his own. The little prince realized his rose was unique because of the time and care he had given her.

6. What was the fox's secret and how did it change the little prince? The fox's secret was that what matters is invisible to the eyes. The little prince understood that he was responsible for his rose forever because he had tamed her.
