解析：as if = as though，表示“仿佛，好像”。此处引导虚拟语气，表示与事实相反或假设的情况。

#### 五、阅读技巧：先重点后细节

英文逻辑：TOPIC AND COMMENT STRUCTURE（话题-评论结构）

先把重点/大信息放在前面，再把细节放在后面。

中文习惯先铺垫再给结论，英文习惯开门见山直接说重点。

例：I know a planet（先说重点：我知道一个星球）

where there is a man with a bright red face（再补充细节：什么样的星球）

掌握这个语序规律，阅读速度会大幅提升。

#### 六、文化背景：存在主义哲学

小王子看 43 次日落，体现的是一种极致的孤独感。

#### 七、故事复述（Chapter 6 & 7）

The Little Prince was feeling sad/lonely, so he liked watching sunsets on his planet. One day, he watched them forty-three times. Later, he asked the narrator if sheep eat flowers with thorns. This led to a discussion about what is truly important/serious. The Little Prince explained that if a sheep ate his flower, it would be like all the stars going out.

#### 三、句法分析

1. 感叹句：How + adj. + 主语+ 谓语!

例：How beautiful you are!（你好漂亮啊！）

例：How exciting it is!（这件事如此振奋人心！）

例：How lovely you are!（你好可爱呀！）

2. 非谓语动词短语做状语（ed/ing 分词）

原文：He stood there confused, holding the glass globe.

解析：confused（过去分词，表示状态：困惑的）+ holding（现在分词，表示伴随动作）

补充信息修饰主句，说明“站在那里”时的状态和伴随动作。

例：She sat there crying, waiting for her boyfriend.

例：He walks through the door, smiling.

3. should have done 句型（本应该做但实际没做）

表示对过去的遗憾和后悔，与事实相反。

例：I should have judged her by her actions, not her words.

例：I never should have run away.

例：I should have guessed the tenderness behind her silly games.

核心句：I was too young to know how to love her.

#### 七、五个描述情感的句型

1. 主语+ could hardly contain + 某人的+ 情绪名词

例：The Little Prince could hardly contain his admiration.

可替换情绪：excitement, sadness, disappointment

2. 主语+ stood/sat there + 状态(ed 分词) + 伴随动作(ing 分词)

例：He stood there confused, holding the glass globe.

3. 主语+ was surprised + that 从句

例：He was surprised she didn't blame him.

4. 主语+ took + 某人的+ 话语+ seriously + and became + 情绪

例：He took her careless remarks seriously and became very unhappy.

5. 主语+ should have judged + 某人+ by + 行动/行为+ not by + 言语

例：People should have judged others by their characters, not by their appearances.

#### 六、故事复述（Chapter 10）

A king lived on asteroid 325, dressed in purple and ermine, seated on a majestic throne. When he saw the Little Prince, he cried “Ah, a subject!” The Little Prince was puzzled — the planet was so tiny, what could a king reign over? The king said everything, for he was not only an absolute monarch but a universal one too. The Little Prince asked for a sunset, and the king consulted a calendar to find a favorable time. When the Little Prince wanted to leave, the king offered him a position as minister of justice or ambassador. The Little Prince thought grown-ups were really peculiar.

#### 二、时态对比：一般过去时 vs 现在完成时 vs 现在进行时

1. 一般过去时（Simple Past）

表示过去某个时间发生的动作，已结束，对现在影响不明确。

例：A beetle arrived from somewhere or other.

2. 现在完成时（Present Perfect）

表示从过去某一时间开始持续到现在的动作/状态，对现在仍有影响。

结构：have/has + 过去分词

例：I've only been interrupted three times in the forty-four years I've lived on this planet.

例：I have lived on this planet for forty-four years.

3. 现在进行时（Present Continuous）

表示当下正在发生的动作。

结构：am/is/are + doing

例：I am managing them. I am counting and recounting them.
